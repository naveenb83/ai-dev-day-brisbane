# Run of day

A full-day template. **Adapt freely** — cut the deep-dive tracks for a
business-heavy room, or extend the labs for a technical one. Times are guides.

## Two tracks after lunch

From Lab 4 onward, let people self-select:

- **Explore track (business / L100–L200):** stay in Genie and AI Functions; do the
  Agent Bricks (no-code) version of Lab 5; focus on *what's possible* and *how to
  spot good use cases*.
- **Build track (technical / L300–L400):** do the code versions of Labs 4–6; go
  deeper on agents, evaluation and shipping.

Announce this at the start so people know both are legitimate.

## Session notes

- **09:15 Foundations:** keep it plain and fast. Use the
  [four-level worked example](../start-here/navigate-by-level.md) to show everyone
  there's a lane for them.
- **11:00 Prompting:** do a live weak→strong demo (steal from
  [Good vs bad examples](../prompt-library/good-vs-bad-examples.md)). Highest
  "aha" per minute of the day.
- **16:30 Governance:** don't skip it even if time is tight — frame it as "how you
  earn the right to put this in front of customers." Two minutes on
  [prompt injection](../production-governance/security.md) lands hard.

## Timing safety valves

- Short on time? Drop Lab 6 (demo it instead) and the second half of Building.
- Room struggling with setup? Extend the 10:00 block; nothing else works until Lab
  0 passes.
- Room racing ahead? Push the stretch goals in each lab.
